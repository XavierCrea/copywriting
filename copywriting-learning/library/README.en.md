# Case library

[English homepage](../../README.md) · [项目中文首页](../../README.zh-CN.md) · [中文案例索引](README.md)

All 45 cases and exercises have English notes and a linked Chinese counterpart. Each page covers source observations, interpretation, application steps, limits, and reported results, followed by an English writing-craft section. Original practice is labeled separately from source quotations. Book references retain the page numbers of the Chinese editions used; English practice is not presented as recovered original advertising copy.

The 18 VeryGoodCopy entries also link to their original English lessons. These pages contain our case analysis and practice, not reproductions of the lessons.

Start from the problem you are trying to write about. From the skill folder, run `python3 scripts/library.py search "pricing objection" --kind cases`. Use `python3 scripts/library.py get M-013 C-shell-fleet-saving-calculation` to open the method and case together. [Searchable catalog](catalog.json)

## The Adweek Copywriting Handbook

- [Fireball pinball](cases/en/C-fireball-question-sequence.md) – Follow the reader's questions from first interest through the concerns that come with owning the product.
- [DC-10 critique and hypothetical rewrite](cases/en/C-dc10-objection-rewrite.md) – Put the reader's actual objection up front and give the evidence that answers it.
- [Scale editing exercise](cases/en/C-scale-editing-exercise.md) – Move the moment of enjoyment earlier, then shorten the draft while keeping its meaning and rhythm.
- [Scale-weight inquiry](cases/en/C-sugarman-scale-buyer-questions.md) – Turn a repeated customer question into a specification that helps buyers decide.
- [Grapefruit mail-order ad](cases/en/C-sugarman-grapefruit-origin-offer.md) – Show the act of selecting the fruit and give the reader a way to try it.
- [Magic Stat thermostat](cases/en/C-sugarman-magicstat-objection-sequence.md) – Address installation concerns early, then explain the controls through daily routines.
- [Swiss Army watch](cases/en/C-sugarman-swatch-choice-context.md) – Lead with one model in an ad and use the catalog for comparing options.
- [APF clock calculator](cases/en/C-sugarman-apf-clock-calculator.md) – Connect a novel feature to a real task before choosing the pitch.
- [Mail-order mansion](cases/en/C-sugarman-mail-order-mansion.md) – Use a story to build interest and give serious prospects a way to take the next step.
- [1976 lantern necklace](cases/en/C-sugarman-1976-commemorative-jewelry.md) – Give a time-sensitive commemorative product a reason to be worn after the occasion.
- [Precor rowing machine](cases/en/C-sugarman-precor-fiction-boundary.md) – Let a specific constraint in the story give the product a reason to appear.
- [Nautilus spelling promotion](cases/en/C-sugarman-nautilus-participation.md) – Invite participation and evaluate purchase intent separately from puzzle engagement.
- [Gold Space Chains](cases/en/C-sugarman-gold-chains-confounds.md) – Make a choice into a humorous story and ask what else may have contributed to the sales result.

## Ogilvy on Advertising

- [Rolls-Royce](cases/en/C-rolls-royce-specific-detail.md) – Make quietness tangible through one sound in a stated driving condition.
- [Dove](cases/en/C-dove-positioning-continuity.md) – Keep one core benefit recognizable across different creative treatments.
- [Rinso](cases/en/C-rinso-useful-information-strategy.md) – Publish a stain-removal guide that gives the reader a reason to keep the ad.
- [Shell fleet oil](cases/en/C-shell-fleet-saving-calculation.md) – Make the higher price comparable through a cost calculation the buyer can redo.
- [Wallachs service](cases/en/C-wallachs-service-evidence.md) – Use a familiar inconvenience, a service record, and a clear policy to show what the customer can expect.
- [Shell pipeline](cases/en/C-shell-pipeline-objection.md) – Start from a shared concern and show evidence from the restored site.
- [Avis](cases/en/C-avis-service-positioning.md) – Turn second place into a reason for better service, then show what that means for a traveler.

- [Warehouse modernization](cases/en/C-zebra-saddle-creek-workflow-proof.md) – Explain a technical product through the work it supports and identify which results belong to the wider solution.

## Influence

- [Samples and gifts](cases/en/C-cialdini-samples-and-gifts.md) – Give the buyer a useful experience before asking them to take the next step.
- [Car quote changed after the buyer committed](cases/en/C-cialdini-car-quote-changed.md) – Examine how a buyer forms reasons for a decision and use that insight with clear, stable terms.
- [Scripted street testimonials](cases/en/C-cialdini-scripted-testimonials.md) – Choose a real customer's experience that answers a prospect's specific question.
- [Sanka coffee ad](cases/en/C-cialdini-sanka-authority.md) – Consider how a speaker's role affects credibility and match their real expertise to the claim.
- [Beef supply notice](cases/en/C-cialdini-beef-supply-notice.md) – Explain a documented supply constraint in terms a purchaser can act on.
- [Cookie availability experiment](cases/en/C-cialdini-cookie-availability.md) – See how low stock, a recent drop in availability, and demand from others shape perceived scarcity.

## VeryGoodCopy Micro-Lessons

These entries include observations, suggested applications, and original English exercises. [Eddie Shleyner's lesson index](https://www.verygoodcopy.com/microlessons)

- [Noticing](cases/en/C-vgc-noticing.md) – Return to a real task and find a detail that answers a reader's question.
- [Horrible, horrible copy](cases/en/C-vgc-horrible-horrible-copy.md) – Check which outcome a reader may infer from a promise and what evidence supports it.
- [Artificial writing](cases/en/C-vgc-artificial-writing.md) – Use firsthand details from interviews to give a draft a recognizable voice.
- [Meeting your firstborn child](cases/en/C-vgc-meeting-your-firstborn-child.md) – Show what changes a person's understanding and how they react.
- [Not doing your job](cases/en/C-vgc-not-doing-your-job.md) – Revisit the same facts from another angle when headline ideas start repeating.
- [Person(a)](cases/en/C-vgc-persona.md) – Start with a real reader's situation and see how often it occurs among similar customers.
- [Predicting the success of an ad campaign](cases/en/C-vgc-predicting-the-success-of-an-ad-campaign.md) – Look at the audience, offer, and creative when diagnosing weak ad performance.
- [Robbing and writing](cases/en/C-vgc-robbing-and-writing.md) – Use a physical action between lines of dialogue to pace a scene and mark a change.
- [Love](cases/en/C-vgc-love.md) – Express a broad feeling through a small moment the reader can recognize.
- [Good copywriting practice](cases/en/C-vgc-good-copywriting-practice.md) – Write for one reader to practice deciding what to explain and what to leave out.
- [Writing immersive stories](cases/en/C-vgc-writing-immersive-stories.md) – Use action and dialogue to build a scene with enough context to follow what happens.
- [Writing, slow](cases/en/C-vgc-writing-slow.md) – Check the claim, evidence, and logical connection behind each polished sentence.
- [A poetic masterpiece](cases/en/C-vgc-a-poetic-masterpiece.md) – Use repetition to connect shared memories to a clear request.
- [Showing vs. telling](cases/en/C-vgc-showing-vs-telling.md) – Replace an abstract description with an action or object that lets the reader picture it.
- [Vignettes](cases/en/C-vgc-vignettes.md) – Expand or compress a single moment and end when the detail has made its point.
- [Benefits](cases/en/C-vgc-benefits.md) – Trace a feature to the outcome that matters to the reader and check each link in the chain.
- [One of the best copywriting exercises](cases/en/C-vgc-one-of-the-best-copywriting-exercises.md) – Shorten a passage and check which facts, conditions, and relationships survive.
- [Writing better dialogue](cases/en/C-vgc-writing-better-dialogue.md) – Read aloud and simplify speech tags while keeping the speaker clear.
