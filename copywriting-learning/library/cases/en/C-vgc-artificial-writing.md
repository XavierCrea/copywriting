# Give a story traceable lived detail | Artificial writing

[English case index](../../README.en.md) · [简体中文](../C-vgc-artificial-writing.md) · Source case and author/publisher account

## Source observations
The author uses a conversation from *Good Will Hunting* to discuss lived experience, while acknowledging that he uses AI for efficiency. His claims about AI capabilities are personal opinions.

## Interpretation and application
Use when fluent brand or customer stories lack the speaker’s own voice. Our application locates distinctiveness in source material and perspective: interviews, actions, and actual wording can give an AI-assisted draft traceable experience.

1. Ask what happened, what the person noticed, and what they said.
2. Choose one firsthand detail relevant to the theme.
3. Mark conjecture for verification and have the participant review the draft.

Check that first-person experience has a real source and that the account remains distinctive without the person’s name.

## Limits and results
Film dialogue illustrates storytelling. This essay is not a model evaluation or controlled marketing test; its technical predictions do not establish current AI capabilities.

Evidence status: Effectiveness unknown.

## English writing craft

**Original practice:** “I kept the first rejected sketch. It reminds me why we moved the handle.” Confirm the sketch and design change before use.

“Kept,” “rejected,” and “moved” carry the story through specific actions. The second sentence gives the retained object a reason to matter. The voice comes from a verifiable choice, not a fabricated confession or a vague claim to passion.
