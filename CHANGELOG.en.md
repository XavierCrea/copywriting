# Changelog

[简体中文](CHANGELOG.md)

## v1.2.2

- Added full English notes for all 45 cases, with writing-craft analysis, labeled original exercises, and links to the Chinese pages and sources.
- Added English titles and tags and bilingual descriptions of common writing problems for 45 cases and 24 methods. Search gives titles and tags more weight than mentions in longer notes.
- Retrieve multiple records in one command and open available case pages from either language.
- Apply reviewed updates with a preview, validation, revision checks, and a backup of the previous catalog.
- Work from confirmed facts through concrete use and conditions. Explore distinct angles for short copy and deliver one main draft for long copy by default.

## v1.1.2

- Expanded the writing guide with practical ways to select information for a reader, develop a benefit, make language concrete, build a short scene, shape rhythm, and tighten copy while preserving meaning.
- Added guidance for diagnosing weak ads and checking what readers might infer from a claim.
- Removed workbench roles and handoff instructions from the skill.
- Added 18 VeryGoodCopy Micro-Lesson notes with sources, conditions for reuse, and English exercises. Both case indexes now cover 45 entries.

## v0.1.2

- Added English versions of the homepage, case index, changelog, and maintainer guide, along with English text in the skill's interface.
- Expanded Getting started to show what to include in a brief. Added examples of English product-page copy and a customer reply based on the Zebra and Saddle Creek case and the scale-weight inquiry case.
- Clarified how to look up a case from an English brief by its ID, brand name, or indexed terms.

## v0.1.1

- Made case-based reasoning part of the writing workflow. Find a relevant case, reuse a technique, revise the draft using current facts, and record traceable feedback when authorized.
- Added walkthroughs for applying cases to a customer reply and an ad headline, including when a second case can help with editing.

## v0.1.0

- Added workflows for drafting, analyzing cases, and learning from feedback across ads, product copy, product pages, marketing emails, and brand copy.
- Included 27 cases and 24 writing methods with sources, application steps, and evidence limits.
- Bundled the searchable library with the skill and made the case pages browsable on GitHub.
