# Changelog

[简体中文](CHANGELOG.md)

## v0.1.2

- Added English versions of the homepage, case index, changelog, and maintainer guide, along with English text in the skill's interface.
- Expanded Getting started to show what to include in a brief. Added examples of English product-page copy and a customer reply based on the Zebra and Saddle Creek case and the scale-weight inquiry case.
- Clarified how to look up a case from an English brief by its ID, brand name, or indexed terms.

## v0.1.1

- Made case-based reasoning part of the writing workflow. Find a relevant case, reuse a technique, revise the draft using current facts, and record traceable feedback when authorized.
- Added walkthroughs for applying cases to a customer reply and an ad headline, including when a second case can help with editing.

## v0.1.0

- Added workflows for drafting, analyzing cases, and learning from feedback across ads, product copy, product pages, marketing emails, and brand copy.
- Included 27 cases and 24 writing methods with sources, application steps, and evidence limits.
- Bundled the searchable library with the skill and made the case pages browsable on GitHub.
